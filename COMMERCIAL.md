# Commercial use

PhotoPhoenix is free under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE) for personal use,
research, education, charities, public-safety and government institutions.

**Any commercial use needs a separate written agreement with the maintainer**, for example:

- offering data-recovery or incident-response services that use PhotoPhoenix,
- bundling it in a product, appliance or paid tool,
- using it inside a company for customers or for the company's own business.

A commercial agreement can include a support and service-level agreement (SLA): response
times, priority fixes, custom variants, and indemnity terms.

Using PhotoPhoenix commercially without an agreement is not permitted by the licence and infringes
the maintainer's copyright, which may be enforced, including claims for damages.

**Contact:** open an issue titled "Commercial licence enquiry" (no confidential details), and
the maintainer will reply with a private contact.
